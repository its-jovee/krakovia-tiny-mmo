# THE PHILOSOPHY

## Dual-Track System

Every devlog runs two parallel tracks:

| Track | Purpose | Viewer Takeaway |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| **Philosophy** | Why this matters beyond games | "This guy gets it" |
| **Progress** | What's actually being built | "This game is real and growing" |

Philosophy hooks non-gamedev viewers. Progress keeps the gamedev audience. Both reinforce each other.

## Core Principles

- **Essay first, features second**: The devlog isn't a patch notes video. It's a reflection that uses the game as evidence.
- **Strong titles, no "devlog" label**: Let the thumbnail contextualize. The title is the thesis.
- **Emotional arc within each video**: Hook → Tension → Insight → Resolution
- **The series itself mirrors the game**: Starts warm and hopeful, ends with acceptance and release
- **The shared experience is the story**: Unlike single-player narratives, this is about what WE built together

# THE PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

For each devlog cycle:

1. **Dev sprint** (6-8 weeks) - Build the version features
2. **Capture week** - Record gameplay, get player footage
3. **Script week** - Write around what you have
4. **Film + edit** (1-2 weeks)
5. **Release → Coincide with version launch**

The devlog announces each version. It's marketing AND documentation AND philosophy.

# DEVLOG 3: "O Jogo Que Ninguém Pediu"

**Pairs with:** V1 Launch (Building System, War Demands, Core NPCs)
**Release:** February-March 2025

---

## Philosophy Track: On Doing Things That Don't Make Sense

**The thesis:** Sometimes the "smart" choice is the wrong choice. Optimization culture tells us to only pursue things with clear ROI. But the best things in life don't have ROI. They have meaning.

**Key points:**
- The game jam loss as "proof" you should quit
- The pressure to be strategic vs. the pull to be authentic
- Doing things because they need to exist, not because they'll succeed
- Building an MMO that's designed to end

**Quotable line:** *"Todo mundo me disse pra fazer um jogo menor. Um jogo 'esperto'. Um jogo que faz sentido pro mercado. Eu entendi o conselho. E ignorei."*

---

## Progress Track: V1 Foundation

**Features to show:**
- [ ] Building system (collective contribution)
- [ ] First buildings going up (Sawmill, Forge, Marketplace)
- [ ] War demands system ("The Front requires...")
- [ ] Maren, Pip, Undertow - the first 3 NPCs
- [ ] Players working together on construction

**The connection:** These systems are "boring" infrastructure. Not flashy. But you're building foundations because you believe in what comes next. That's the unsexy part of any meaningful project.

# DEVLOG 4: "Por Que Você Não Lembra de Nenhum NPC"

**Pairs with:** V1 (full NPC cast milestone)
**Release:** March-April 2025

---

## Philosophy Track: On Presence vs. Function

**The thesis:** We optimize everything for function. NPCs exist to give quests. Coworkers exist to complete tasks. Even friends become "networking." But presence - just being with someone - is where connection happens.

**Key points:**
- Most NPCs are quest dispensers, not characters
- Efficiency culture bleeding into storytelling
- The radical act of making someone just... exist
- Why we remember some characters and forget others

**Quotable line:** *"Pensa no último NPC que você encontrou em um jogo. Você lembra o nome dele? A voz? Alguma coisa além da quest que ele deu?"*

**The connection:** These NPCs don't exist to serve you. They exist. Period. That's why you'll care about them when things get hard.

# DEVLOG 5: "Sozinho Você Morre"

**Pairs with:** V2 Launch (Governance, Banking, Market Visibility)
**Release:** May-June 2025

---

## Philosophy Track: On Needing People

**The thesis:** Independence is overrated. We're taught self-sufficiency as the ultimate goal. But the best moments require other people. Not want - require. There's freedom in admitting you can't do it alone.

**Key points:**
- Hyper-independence as trauma response, not strength
- Games can model healthy interdependence
- The vulnerability of asking for help
- Why necessity creates deeper bonds than choice

**Quotable line:** *"A gente passa a vida inteira tentando não precisar de ninguém. E se precisar fosse o ponto?"*

## Progress Track: Governance & Economy

**Key points:**
- Intuition as valid knowledge
- The slow creep of things falling apart
- How we explain away warning signs
- Trusting yourself when something feels off

**Quotable line:** *"Você já sentiu que algo tava errado, mas não sabia explicar o quê? A gente aprende a ignorar isso. E se a gente não devesse?"*

---

## The Concept

This isn't a scripted devlog. It's a documentary of the actual ending. Real final day. Real players. Real goodbye.

---

## The Event (December 31, 2025)

Announce the exact time. "Kraftovia ends at [time] on December 31st."

**What you capture:**
- Final hours, players gathering
- In-game chat, what they're saying
- The monument, complete
- Discord calls, real voices, real goodbyes
- You playing alongside them, one last time
- Maren's speech
- The countdown
- The server shutdown
- Silence

---

## Structure

**[0:00-0:30] COLD OPEN**
Server logs. Player count: [X]. Timestamp: December 31, 2025, 23:55. Black.

**[0:30-3:00] HOOK + CONTEXT**
*"Há um ano, eu comecei um MMO que ia morrer. Todo mundo disse que ninguém ia jogar um jogo que acaba."*
*"Eles estavam errados."*
*"Esse vídeo é sobre o dia que Kraftovia morreu. Com [X] jogadores online. Do jeito que tinha que ser."*

Quick montage: the year, the devlogs, the community, the story.

**[3:00-8:00] THE FINAL DAY**
Real footage. Minimal narration. Let it breathe.
- Players logging in from everywhere
- Chat messages: "I can't believe it's ending"
- Visiting NPCs one last time
- Someone at Undertow's fishing spot, just sitting
- Old players returning: "I had to be here for this"
- The city full for the last time

**[8:00-10:00] THE GATHERING**
All players converge at the monument.
Discord audio layered in. Real voices.
Someone in chat: "Thank you for making this."
Players doing the gesture - the promise - together.

**[10:00-12:00] MAREN'S SPEECH**
In-game. The final moment.
*"You built something real here. All of you. That matters."*
*"Promise me you'll stretch out your hand. And ask to be friends. Again."*

**[12:00-13:00] THE COUNTDOWN**
"Dez minutos."
"Cinco minutos."
"Um minuto."
"Obrigado por jogarem."
Black screen.

**[13:00-15:00] THE SILENCE**
Hold on black. 5 seconds. 10. Uncomfortable.
Discord aftermath. Real reactions.
"It's really gone."
"I feel so weird right now."
"That was beautiful."
Screenshots: "Server offline."

**[15:00-17:00] THE THESIS**
*"Kraftovia durou 12 meses. [X mil] jogadores passaram por ele."*
*"[X] edifícios foram construídos. [X] demandas de guerra foram cumpridas. [X] heróis morreram."*
*"O jogo acabou. Mas a experiência não."*
*"Steam. Story Mode. Pra quem quer viver a memória. Sandbox. Pra quem quer construir de novo."*

**[17:00-18:00] THE PROMISE**
*"Maren pediu uma promessa. Pra estender a mão e pedir pra ser amigo de novo."*
*"Ela não tava falando do jogo."*
Beat.
*"Valeu por jogar. Valeu por ficar até o fim."*
*"Agora vai lá fora e cumpre a promessa."*
Black.

**[18:00+] CREDITS**
- Player screenshots/messages
- Memorial montage (all player names who played)
- Statistics: buildings built, gold traded, demands met
- NPC farewell quotes
- Post-credits: Steam trailer

# THE META-ARC

The devlog series mirrors the game:

| Devlog | Game Phase | Tone |
|--------|-----------|------|
| 3 | V1: Foundation | Defiant hope |
| 4 | V1: NPCs | Warmth, charm |
| 5 | V2: Governance | Community, energy |
| 6 | V2: Economy | Depth, complexity |
| 7 | V3: Strain | Unease, pressure |
| 8 | V3-V4: Fall | Melancholy, acceptance |
| 9 | V4: Final | Release, hope |
| Final | Shutdown | Catharsis, closure |

Viewers who watch all of them experience the same emotional arc as players.

# KEY DIFFERENCES FROM ORIGINAL PLAN

| Original | Updated |
|----------|---------|
| Per-player NPC disappearances | Global war casualties + NPC stress |
| Account deletion ending | Shared server shutdown |
| Layer system (emptying world) | Shared world, everyone sees same state |
| 15-month / 7 devlogs | 12-month / 8 devlogs |
| Update 0.1-0.7 | V1-V4 |
| Solo emotional journey | Shared community journey |

**The core philosophy tracks remain identical.** The delivery mechanism changed to match our shared-world approach.
